Point at your app. Say what to change.

Pointbrief is a dev toolbar for your running app. Talk, point and draw on the page the way you would in a Loom, and it hands your coding agent a brief: every change you asked for, in your words, tied to the exact element, selector and source file.

How it works

Typing "the button on the right is too small" into a chat box makes the agent guess which button. Pointing at it doesn't.

1

Record over your app

Press Record on the toolbar and use your app as usual. Talk the way you'd talk to a developer looking over your shoulder, or type notes.

2

Point and draw

Hover or click what you mean. Switch to Mark to circle things, underline copy, cross out or scribble over what should go, or draw an arrow from where something is to where it belongs.

3

Hand it to your agent

Press Done. Each thought becomes one item with your words and its targets. Copy it into any agent, or send it to Claude Code, which edits the code and shows you the diff before anything is committed.

Why the brief is precise

Agents can't watch a video. A brief gives them what a developer would work out from one.

Mapped to elements

Every target carries a short unique CSS selector, its visible text, role, current styles and position. In React, Vue and Svelte dev builds it also names the component and the source file, React 19 included.

Words lined up with pointing

"Make this bigger and this one blue" becomes "make this [1] bigger and this [2] one blue", with [1] and [2] pointing at the two elements you were on when you said them.

Gestures mean something

A circle marks a region; a strike-through, cross or scribble means remove; an underline usually means the wording; an arrow means move this there. A mark with no words still becomes an item.

Emphasis becomes priority

"Way too small", "really", repeating yourself or circling twice marks an item high priority, with the reason, so the agent does it first.

Install

The toolbar is one script for your app in development. The plugin lets Claude Code receive briefs directly. Use either or both.

Claude Code

In Claude Code, add the marketplace and install the plugin:

/plugin marketplace add theguysaccount/loom-to-code-vibe-coding
/plugin install pointbrief@pointbrief

Then run /pointbrief:setup to add the toolbar to your app, and /pointbrief:apply after you send a brief. Needs Node.js 18+. Details

What stays on your machine

  • No screen video and no screenshots: the toolbar records events (what you pointed at, what you drew, what you said), not pixels.
  • It never reads form fields or passwords, and anything inside data-pointbrief-private is left out.
  • Voice is transcribed by your browser. Chrome does it on the device when it has the language pack; otherwise browsers use their own speech service, and the toolbar tells you which before you start. Prefer typing? Choose Type instead.
  • The brief leaves the page only when you copy it, download it, or send it to the plugin on 127.0.0.1.
